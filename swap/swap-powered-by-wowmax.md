# Swap (Powered by WOWMAX)

&#x20;GFEX provides a swap function powered by [WOWMAX](https://wowmax.exchange), one of the leading DEX aggregators and our official partner.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

&#x20;GFEX's swap function lets you exchange your assets for others at the best possible rate with low gas fees.

&#x20;Currently, GFEX's swap function is only available on the Base mainnet, but it will support more networks as we continue to expand.
