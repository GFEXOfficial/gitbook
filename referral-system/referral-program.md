# Referral Program

## 1. Referral Program

GFEX offers a referral program that rewards you with 5% of your referrals’ trading fees.

Payouts are distributed daily when the total amount exceeds 1 USDC. If the amount is below 1 USDC, it will be accumulated and paid out once it surpasses 1 USDC.

All payouts are automatically processed by a smart contract, ensuring full transparency and immutability on the blockchain.

## 2. How to Join the Referral Program

#### A. Navigate to your wallet address and select it.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (10).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### B. Next, navigate to the My Page section to access your referral page.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (11).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### C. Click the 'Referral' tab, and the referral page will appear.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (12).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once you copy your referral link, share it with others. When someone joins using your link, you'll automatically receive 5% of their trading fees, distributed directly to your wallet.
