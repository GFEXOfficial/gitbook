# Allowance Setting



### 1. What is Token Approval?

Token approval is a security feature that allows smart contracts to spend tokens on your behalf. You must approve the contract before it can transfer your tokens.



### 2. Why is it Necessary?

This two-step process (approve + transfer) protects your tokens from unauthorized access. Only contracts you explicitly approve can move your tokens.



### 3. Gas Fee Savings Approving a larger amount reduces transaction fees.&#x20;

Instead of paying gas fees multiple times, approve once for multiple transactions. For example, approving $5,000 allows you to invest multiple times without additional approval fees.



⚠️ Security Note Unlimited approval is convenient but carries higher risk. Only approve unlimited amounts for trusted contracts. You can always revoke or reduce approval later.
