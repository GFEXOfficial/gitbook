# GFEX Tutorial (Mobile)

## **Reminder (Important!)**

**To ensure optimal security and performance on GFEX, we recommend using the** [**MetaMask**](https://metamask.io/download/) **app on your mobile device. GFEX is designed to run within** [**MetaMask**](https://metamask.io/download/)**'s secure browser.**



### **1. Connect Your Wallet**

To start, connect your wallet. You'll find the "Connect Wallet" button in the top-left corner menu.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/1.jpg" alt="" width="296"><figcaption><p>Click the menu in the top-left corner.</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/2.jpg" alt="" width="296"><figcaption><p>Click 'Connect Wallet' button to connect your wallet.</p></figcaption></figure>

### 2. Authorize the Connection

After initiating the wallet connection, you'll need to sign and approve the connection of your wallet to GFEX for investment. This authorization step is required only once. Afterward, no additional signatures will be needed for connection authorization unless you sign out.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/3.jpg" alt="" width="296"><figcaption><p>Choose 'Metamsk' (strongly recommended).</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/4.jpg" alt="" width="296"><figcaption><p>Click 'Connect' to authorize.</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/5.jpg" alt="" width="296"><figcaption><p>Click 'Sign message' for authorization.</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/6.jpg" alt="" width="296"><figcaption><p>Once connected, You will see you wallet address.</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/7.jpg" alt="" width="296"><figcaption><p>Your balance, trading history, and a link to the block <br>explorer will appear when you click your address.</p></figcaption></figure>

### 3. Select Investment Options and Signing for the Investment.

Select your desired investment amount ($3, $5, $25, or $50), and your market prediction (<mark style="color:red;">Widen</mark> or <mark style="color:blue;">Narrow</mark>). After confirming these details, a signature request will appear in your wallet to finalize your investment. Your investment will then be executed with a duration of one minute.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/8.jpg" alt="" width="296"><figcaption><p>Choose the investment amount.</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/9.jpg" alt="" width="296"><figcaption><p>Singing for the investment confirmation.</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/10.jpg" alt="" width="296"><figcaption><p>Sign for the spending cap approval.</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/11.jpg" alt="" width="296"><figcaption><p>Another signing for the gas spending approval.</p></figcaption></figure>

### 4. Await the Results

After your investment is confirmed, wait 1 minute for the result.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/12.jpg" alt="" width="296"><figcaption><p>Once position opened, wait 1 minute.</p></figcaption></figure>

### 5. Getting Result

Once the selected duration ends, the results will be displayed. Any applicable [fees](../investment/how-proceeds-are-calculated.md#id-3.-example-fee-application-and-profit-distribution) will be deducted, and profits will be processed accordingly.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/13.jpg" alt="" width="296"><figcaption><p>Result will be shown after 1 minute.</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/14.jpg" alt="" width="296"><figcaption><p>Close the result pop-up, and you'll find the closing <br>Gap Index. You can then click <strong>"Invest Again"</strong> <br>for the next round.</p></figcaption></figure>
