# Using Web3 Platform

### Getting Started with Web3 Platforms: Installing and Using MetaMask

Web3 technology brings a decentralized internet powered by blockchain. To interact with Web3 platforms, you’ll need a Web3 wallet. MetaMask is one of the most popular and user-friendly Web3 wallets available. Below is a step-by-step guide to installing MetaMask and connecting it to a Web3 platform.

***

### **1. Installing MetaMask**

MetaMask is available as a browser extension or mobile app.

**a. Installing the Browser Extension**

1. Visit the [official MetaMask website](https://metamask.io/).
2. Click the “Download” button and choose the extension for your browser (e.g., Chrome, Firefox).
3. Add MetaMask to your browser and complete the installation.

**b. Installing the Mobile App**

1. Open the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android) on your mobile device.
2. Search for “MetaMask” and download the app.

***

### 2. **Creating and Setting Up Your MetaMask Wallet**

**a. Creating a New Wallet**

1. Open MetaMask and select the “Create a Wallet” option.
2. Set a password and store it securely.
3. Write down the Secret Recovery Phrase (seed phrase) provided by MetaMask and keep it in a safe place. This phrase is essential for recovering your wallet and must never be shared.

**b. Restoring an Existing Wallet**

If you already have a wallet, choose the “Import Wallet” option and enter your Secret Recovery Phrase to restore it.

***

### 3. **Connecting MetaMask to a Web3 Platform**&#x20;

**a. Wallet Connection**

1. Go to the Web3 platform (e.g., [GFEX](https://www.gfex.io/)).
2. Click on the “Connect Wallet” button, usually found at the top of the page or in the settings menu.
3. Select MetaMask, review the requested permissions, and approve the connection.

**b. Checking Network Settings**

* Web3 platforms may require specific blockchain networks (e.g., Ethereum Mainnet or Base).
* In MetaMask, open the “Networks” menu, select the required network, or manually add it if necessary.
* **For GFEX users**, the platform will automatically switch your network to Base when you connect your wallet, ensuring a seamless experience.

**c. Executing Transactions**

1. Set up your desired transaction on the Web3 platform (e.g., Investing on [GFEX](https://www.gfex.io/)).
2. When prompted, MetaMask will display a pop-up for transaction confirmation.
3. Review the details and click “Confirm” to submit the transaction to the blockchain.

***

### **4. Important Tips for Safe Usage**

1. **Keep Your Wallet Secure**
   * Never share your Secret Recovery Phrase with anyone.
   * Lock or log out of MetaMask when not in use.
2. **Check Transaction Fees**
   * Blockchain transactions involve gas fees. Always review the estimated fees in the MetaMask pop-up before confirming.
3. **Verify URLs**
   * Use only the official URLs of Web3 platforms to avoid phishing scams.

***

### **Conclusion**

MetaMask and other Web3 wallets are essential tools for accessing the decentralized internet. With just a few simple steps, you can set up MetaMask and start using platforms like [GFEX](https://www.gfex.io/) to trade securely and efficiently.

Get started with MetaMask today and explore the limitless possibilities of the Web3 world!
